# Handoffs

## Sequence

```text
1. Business model review identifies the recurring manual workflows.
2. Research work maps the most common operational pain points.
3. Use-case design turns those pain points into AgencyOS value opportunities.
4. Risk review trims the model to what is realistic and safe.
5. Recommendation memo defines the most likely ROI and pilot path.
```

## Handoff packet

```text
- business model summary
- manual pain points
- process or communication bottlenecks
- operational risk and human guardrails
- recommended value opportunity
```
