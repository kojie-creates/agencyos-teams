# Governance Notes

## Core operating constraints

```text
- Keep the project grounded in local youth sports operations, not a software-first fantasy.
- Focus on problems that are repetitive, labor-intensive, and operationally important.
- Do not assume full automation of player experience, coaching quality, or field logistics.
- Human approval remains required for parent communications, staffing decisions, and schedule changes.
```

## Risk boundaries

```text
- volunteer and staff coordination must stay accountable to real people
- parent communication can become a service problem if not structured
- rescheduling and weather issues require clear operational ownership
- marketing automation should not replace relationship quality
```
