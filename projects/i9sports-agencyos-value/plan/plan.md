# Project Plan

## Project Goal

```text
Determine whether a successful, manual youth sports operator can benefit from AgencyOS orchestration, workflow design, and operational automation.
```

## Workstream Map

| Workstream | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business model review | Understand how i9 Sports operates and where the business is manual. | Planned |
| Operational pain mapping | Identify scheduling, parent comms, staff coordination, and reporting friction. | Planned |
| Use-case design | Define realistic AgencyOS value in recurring operations. | Planned |
| Risk and governance | Separate safe automation from operational or customer-facing risk. | Planned |
| Recommendation memo | Summarize strategic fit and likely ROI pathways. | Planned |

## Likely Operational Pain Points

```text
- parent registration and follow-up
- league schedule creation and changes
- coach recruitment and onboarding
- field/venue and official coordination
- weather/reschedule communication
- performance reporting and retention loop
- seasonal marketing and re-registration campaigns
```
