# Research Workstream

## Goal

```text
Understand the business model and identify where a successful youth sports organization could be manual, repetitive, or administratively overloaded.
```

## Website-based findings

```text
i9 Sports is positioned as a youth sports league provider focused on fun, age-appropriate participation, and team sportsmanship. The site highlights features such as no tryouts, all-skill levels welcome, one-day-per-week programming, and trained officials.
```

## Operational implications

```text
Those program features imply a heavy amount of scheduling, schedule communication, venue coordination, parent expectations management, and recurring seasonal execution. Any business with this model can become manual very quickly as volume grows.
```

## Value lens

```text
The right AgencyOS question is not 'can we replace sports operations.' It is 'where can manual coordination be turned into a repeatable operating system without removing the personal element of local coaching and family experience.'
```
