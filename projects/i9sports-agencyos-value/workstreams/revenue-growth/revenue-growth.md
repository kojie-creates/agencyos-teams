# Revenue and Retention Workstream

## Goal

```text
Assess how AgencyOS could improve retention, conversion, and operating leverage in a youth sports program business.
```

## Likely value levers

```text
- more timely parent communications
- smoother seasonal re-registration campaigns
- better coach retention and staffing visibility
- improved parent satisfaction through consistent follow-up
- less lost time from manual coordination and missed tasks
```

## Recommendation

```text
AgencyOS is most likely to create value as a retention and operational leverage tool rather than as a front-end growth engine by itself. The gains come from reducing the cost of managing the business well.
```
