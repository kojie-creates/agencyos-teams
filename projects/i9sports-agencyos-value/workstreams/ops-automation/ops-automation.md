# Operations Automation Workstream

## Goal

```text
Map recurring youth sports operating tasks that could be standardized, delegated, or coordinated more effectively with AgencyOS.
```

## Use cases

```text
- registration intake and missing information follow-up
- parent communication templates and reminders
- schedule build and reschedule management
- coach and official assignment tracking
- venue coordination and equipment checklists
- attendance, no-show, and follow-up tracking
- weather or cancellation workflows
- seasonal recap and next-season re-engagement
```

## What AgencyOS would likely improve

```text
AgencOS would help by converting ad hoc work into visible operating handoffs, standard templates, status tracking, and consistent follow-through. This matters in a service business where organization and communication drive parent trust.
```
