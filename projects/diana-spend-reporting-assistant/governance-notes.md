## Project-Specific Rules

```text
This is an internal first-run setup.
Customer briefs are source material, not proof of the actual implementation.
The current pipeline is described as likely or apparent unless verified from code, logs, architecture docs, or dev-team confirmation.
Diana's judgment remains the authority for spend interpretation.
Deterministic code, SQL, spreadsheet logic, or rules engines should own financial calculations.
AI may draft narrative only after metrics are verified.
```

## External-Facing Boundaries

```text
Do not send customer, dev-team, or executive messages without Kojie's approval.
Do not claim guaranteed accuracy, cost savings, productivity gains, compliance, or legal correctness.
Do not request or process sensitive client spend data without explicit permission and handling rules.
Do not imply AgencyOS replaces Diana's judgment or the firm's approval process.
```

## Evidence Requirements

```text
Source claims must point to one of the three customer PDFs or existing internal Diana docs.
Implementation claims require direct technical evidence.
Financial-metric claims require deterministic calculation evidence and reconciliation notes.
Executive claims must be labeled as supported, inferred, assumed, unknown, or unsupported.
```

## Human Approval Gates

```text
External sends.
Client-facing deliverables.
Requests for source data.
Use of sensitive matter, invoice, client, or vendor information.
Paid commitments.
Legal, financial, compliance, or production architecture commitments.
Memory or governance changes.
```
