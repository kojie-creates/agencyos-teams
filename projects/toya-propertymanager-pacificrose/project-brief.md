# Project Brief

## Project Name

```text
Toya Property Manager Pacific Rose
```

## Primary Audience

```text
Toya, property manager.
```

## Employer / Stakeholder

```text
Cirrus Asset Management.
```

## Outcome

```text
Create a property-operations use case showing how AgencyOS Teams could help organize Pacific Rose around maintenance, tenant communication, leasing visibility, vendor coordination, risk escalation, reporting, and learning.
```

## Why This Needs Teams

```text
The work spans tenants, rent status, maintenance, vendors, utilities, parking, laundry, waste, refurbishment history, management transition history, tenant sentiment, owner/employer reporting, and housing/legal risk. One assistant is too narrow; Teams can route the work across coordinated lanes.
```

## Value Hypothesis

```text
AgencyOS Teams can help Toya convert scattered property issues into visible intake, prioritized maintenance, tenant communication logs, vendor handoffs, risk flags, owner-ready reporting, and repeatable operating rhythms.
```

## Non-Goals

```text
Do not give legal advice.
Do not make rent, eviction, habitability, utility, or vendor decisions.
Do not contact tenants, Toya, Cirrus, vendors, or ownership without approval.
Do not present source-provided facts as verified.
Do not use tenant-sensitive details in outreach without explicit approval.
```
