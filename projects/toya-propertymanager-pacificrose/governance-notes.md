# Governance Notes

Use the shared standard:

```text
../../governance/GOVERNANCE-STANDARD.md
```

## Project-Specific Rules

```text
Treat all property details as source-provided until verified.
Tenant-sensitive information must be minimized.
Rent status, mold, maintenance neglect, habitability, utility billing, and lease terms are sensitive and may involve legal/housing risk.
AgencyOS Teams may prepare internal drafts and checklists, but may not make housing, legal, rent, vendor, or employer decisions.
```

## External-Facing Boundaries

```text
No outreach to Toya, Cirrus Asset Management, tenants, vendors, ownership, or agencies without approval.
No public-facing claim about Pacific Rose without verification.
No tenant-specific claim unless the human explicitly approves the wording and use.
No legal, rent, eviction, habitability, health, or safety recommendation.
```

## Evidence Requirements

```text
Verified property claims require source documents or direct confirmation.
Operational recommendations require clear assumptions.
Financial claims require rent roll, expense data, vacancy data, delinquency data, and approved calculation method.
Maintenance and mold claims require records, photos, inspection reports, or documented tickets before external use.
```

## Human Approval Gates

```text
Toya-facing notes.
Cirrus-facing pilot brief.
Tenant survey or outreach.
Vendor contact.
Any property condition claim.
Any rent, lease, legal, habitability, utility, safety, or financial conclusion.
Any memory update.
```
