# AgencyOS Teams Value Hypothesis

Audience:

```text
Internal draft for Kojie.
```

## Plain Value Statement

```text
AgencyOS Teams could help Toya make Pacific Rose operations visible, prioritized, and easier to report by routing tenant issues, maintenance needs, vendor handoffs, risk flags, and owner/employer updates through a governed AI operating layer.
```

## What AgencyOS Teams Would Do

```text
Capture tenant and property issues.
Classify urgency and risk.
Route work to maintenance, vendor, management, or evidence review lanes.
Track open items and aging.
Prepare tenant-safe communication drafts.
Prepare Toya/Cirrus weekly operating briefs.
Separate verified facts from assumptions.
Escalate legal, housing, safety, mold, rent, or proprietary-data issues.
Learn recurring patterns over time.
```

## What It Would Not Do

```text
Replace Toya.
Make legal or housing decisions.
Promise rent recovery.
Promise tenant satisfaction.
Send tenant or employer messages without approval.
Treat unverified tenant claims as facts.
Override Cirrus policy.
```

## Strongest Demo Angle

```text
Pacific Rose has many operational signals: long-term tenants, maintenance visibility complaints, mixed unit condition, laundry/vendor dependencies, assigned parking, waste/recycling constraints, rent-status variation, and management-transition history. AgencyOS Teams can show value by making those signals organized and actionable.
```

## Public Online Presence Angle

```text
Pacific Rose already has a public leasing site with resident/applicant portals, office hours, amenities, and Cirrus management information. AgencyOS Teams could help connect that public-facing promise to internal operating visibility: resident issues, maintenance follow-through, vendor status, risk flags, and weekly reporting.
```

## Resident Portal Maintenance Angle

```text
A saved Pacific Rose RENTCafe maintenance page confirms resident-facing Submit Maintenance Request and Request History tabs. That gives AgencyOS Teams a practical first operating surface: organize request intake, history review, aging, unresolved themes, follow-up drafts, and weekly maintenance visibility without inventing a new tenant workflow.
```

## Default Failure Categories

```text
The first maintenance visibility view should default to likely property-failure categories before generic alphabetical review: Plumbing, Appliances, Water Intrusion/Drywall, Heating & Cooling, HVAC, Doors and Windows, Electrical, Pest Control, Flooring, and Painting.
These defaults are an operating hypothesis based on Pacific Rose's older-building context, lived-in unit wear, hot shower needs, dishwasher replacement, garbage disposal maintenance, and recurring apartment-life failures.
```

## Suggested First Safe Demo

```text
Create a Pacific Rose maintenance visibility and weekly operations brief template:
- open maintenance themes
- default failure category view
- request-history status themes
- tenant communication themes
- vendor issues
- public portal / online presence observations
- vacancy and rent-status snapshot
- condition/risk items
- evidence needed
- decisions needed from Toya or Cirrus
```

## Evidence Label

```text
This is a value hypothesis, not a verified performance claim.
```
