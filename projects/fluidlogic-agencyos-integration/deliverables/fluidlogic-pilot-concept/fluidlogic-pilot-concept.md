# FluidLogic Pilot Concept

## Recommended pilot wedge

High-heat endurance or high-load performance athletes, with a coach or performance team involved.

## Why this wedge

- It aligns directly with FluidLogic’s public performance narrative.
- It preserves the race-proven credibility bridge.
- It is narrow enough for a small team to test.
- It allows proof to be gathered through a focused set of athlete conversations and a limited commercial test.

## Pilot structure

- Segment: 10–20 athletes or small team environments
- Duration: 60–90 days
- Buyer path: athlete, coach, trainer, or team performance lead
- Offer: limited pilot access, assessment, and proof-backed onboarding
- Measure: qualified conversations, interest conversion, continuation rate, and proof feedback

## Pilot success metrics

- Number of qualified introductions
- Pilot participation rate
- Continued interest after the test window
- Quality of coach or team feedback
- Number of referrals or expansion opportunities

## Messaging frame for outreach

- “Performance hydration for athletes operating in high-load environments.”
- “Built from motorsports-grade performance thinking.”
- “Designed for athletes who need hydration strategy under pressure.”

## Risk guardrail

Do not imply guaranteed gains or universal athlete benefit. The pilot should position itself as a controlled proof-and-learning exercise, not a broad performance claim campaign.
