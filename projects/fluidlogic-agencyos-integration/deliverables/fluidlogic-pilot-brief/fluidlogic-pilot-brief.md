# FluidLogic Pilot Brief

## Pilot Objective

```text
Run a narrow, measurable athlete pilot that proves FluidLogic can enter the elite athlete market with premium, performance-first positioning and a credible proof-backed offer.
```

## Recommended Pilot Scope

```text
Pilot one high-value wedge, not the entire market.
Example: a limited set of athletes in a heat-heavy endurance or training environment with a coach or performance partner involved.
```

## Pilot Design

```text
- narrow segment
- short duration
- defined buyer and influencer
- success metrics agreed in advance
- weekly learning review
```

## Metrics

```text
- number of qualified conversations
- conversion to paid pilot or sample distribution
- referral quality
- repeat use or continuation interest
- clarity of proof requirements
```

## Risk Guardrail

```text
Do not promise broad recovery, medical outcomes, or guaranteed performance gains without source-backed evidence and human review.
```
