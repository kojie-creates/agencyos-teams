## 3. Conversation guide (15 minutes)

1. What do they ride, in what heat, with what helmet?  
2. How do they drink now? (hose / bottle / nothing / pit only)  
3. Have they taken a hand off to drink in a race? What happened?  
4. Water-only — deal-breaker?  
5. 2.6 kg wet on the back — deal-breaker?  
6. Can they do one heat session + a weekly check-in this month?  
7. Loan vs they buy (Juan decides the offer before the call).  
8. Stop. Recap. No "you'll feel sharper."

**If they ask "does it make me faster?"**  
"We're not claiming that. We're testing whether you actually use the button and would race with it again."

**If they ask about Reddick / Palou / NASCAR:**  
"That's the vehicle system (VMR), different product. This block is GPR50 on two wheels. We don't use those wins as proof for the pack."
