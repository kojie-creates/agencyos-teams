## FAQ (send with the invite or after a yes)

**What is GPR50?**  
A FluidLogic wearable: 50oz pack, in-helmet boom, wireless remote on bar or clutch, haptic prompt. The app can estimate a hydration plan. It is not a sports drink.

**What does it cost?**  
List $650. This block is [loan / 30-day trial / purchase — Juan circles]. Military/first-responder discount exists on the public product page; this block may or may not use it.

**What do I put in it?**  
Clean water. Mix, electrolytes, or other fluids can void the warranty. If you must run mix in the pack, this block is not for you.

**Will it fit my helmet?**  
Built for most open- and full-face helmets. Magnetic connection, no permanent cut. If it won't fit, you're out of the block — tell us.

**How heavy?**  
About 2.5 lb empty, about 5.75 lb with 50oz in it. If that's a no, say no.

**Battery?**  
USB-C, about 2 hours to charge. Company says drink-only mode is long; a full charge covers multiple fill-and-dispense cycles. Check the LED or the app.

**Does it keep water cold?**  
Not an insulated pack. Start with cold water or ice.

**Will this make me faster / safer / sharper?**  
We are not claiming that. We are testing whether you use the button and would race with it again.

**Is this the NASCAR / IndyCar system?**  
No. That's VMR, in the car. This block is GPR50 on two wheels. We don't use those race results as proof for the pack.

**Can I post that FluidLogic made me win?**  
No. Don't publish performance claims from this block. Notes stay internal until we close the pilot.

**What series is this aimed at?**  
Two-wheel heat: moto, enduro, SCORE/Baja two-wheel, GNCC, serious MTB. Not running, not football.

**How long?**  
4–6 weeks or one race block, whichever is shorter. Weekly 10-minute check-in.

**What happens if I hate it?**  
You say so, in your words. Weight, water-only, and "not worth $650" are useful answers. We'd rather have that than a fake quote.
