# FluidLogic Market Entry Wedge Options

## Wedge Strategy

```text
The winning entry wedge should transfer race-proven credibility into an athlete environment where hydration timing and performance under pressure are critical, but the audience is not being served by generic wellness hydration brands.
```

## Ranked Options

1. High-heat endurance athletes
   - Example: triathlon, long-course runners, cyclists, ultra athletes
   - Proof transfer: hydration delivery under sustained exertion and heat
   - Buyer: athlete, coach, sponsor, performance staff

2. Team-sport and performance training segments
   - Example: football, soccer, tennis, combat sports, training blocks
   - Proof transfer: hydration timing under repeated high-intensity sessions
   - Buyer: team performance staff, trainer, medical/performance director

3. Motor-sport adjacent high-load athletes
   - Example: mountain biking, motocross, off-road endurance, tactical operators
   - Proof transfer: direct sports-science alignment with motorsports environments
   - Buyer: athlete, manager, coach, sponsor

4. Heat-exposed tactical and field athletes
   - Example: field operators, tactical training, physically demanding environments
   - Proof transfer: high-heat delivery and decision support under load
   - Buyer: team lead, performance staff

## Recommendation

```text
Start with the athlete segment where FluidLogic's proof is most obviously transferable and where a small team can test with clear commercial signals.
```
