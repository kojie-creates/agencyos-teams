## Approved language (athlete-facing, if Juan proceeds)

- race-proven **systems** (VMR / GPR50), not race-proven **outcomes**
- intelligent / strategic hydration as **product name / method**, not as a clinical result
- low-distraction / hands-on-controls access
- heat, duration, focus, timing
- water delivered at the button / interval
- company-reported Baja field observations (with "not a clinical trial")
- rider reviews as reviews

- wellness, hydration journey, feel your best, balance, self-care, biohack
- guaranteed gains, clinically proven for athletes, prevents injury
- we make you faster / we won NASCAR / IndyCar
- elite athlete hydration leader
- electrolytes-in-pack (conflicts with warranty)
- GPR50-for-marathon / GPR50-for-football without a different SKU
