# Governance Notes

## Strategic Constraints

```text
- Never position FluidLogic as a generic hydration lifestyle brand.
- Preserve race-proven credibility and translate it into athlete language.
- Keep the company in premium performance, not wellness vibes.
- Require evidence for every public-facing claim.
- Keep the work executable by a small team.
```

## Risk Boundaries

```text
- Claims about dehydration, performance, safety, or recovery must be tied to a source.
- Any athlete-facing promise needs human signoff.
- Pilot plans should be bounded and observable before acceleration.
- Messaging should not imply medical treatment or broad physiological guarantees.
```

## Human Gate

```text
Juan and/or the designated commercial owner should approve target segment selection, public messaging, pilot scope, and launch activation.
```
