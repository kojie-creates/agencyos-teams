# Project Plan

## Project Goal

```text
Operationalize a research-to-GTM workflow for FluidLogic's transition from motorsports and tactical performance into elite athlete demand.
```

## Workstream Map

| Workstream | Purpose | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intake & Routing | Classify work and map the sequence of agency steps. | @team-lead + central-orchestrator | Planned |
| Research | Map competition, athlete pain, wedge options, and evidence gaps. | research-coordinator | Planned |
| Positioning | Translate race-proven credibility into premium athlete positioning. | distribution-coordinator + marketing-strategy | Planned |
| Pilot Design | Build the first 90-day commercial test. | learning-coordinator + experimentation | Planned |
| Sales Enablement | Turn positioning into proof, objection handling, and outreach materials. | distribution-coordinator | Planned |
| Launch Ops | Activate launch cadence and engagement operations. | distribution-coordinator + community-manager | Planned |
| Learning Loop | Track pilot response and refine GTM strategy. | learning-coordinator + analytics | Planned |
| Evidence Review | Validate claims before client-facing use. | truth-agent / risk-and-proof | Planned |

## Safe Parallel Work

```text
- research the elite athlete landscape
- classify wedge opportunities by buyer, proof burden, and transferability
- draft positioning pillars tied to evidence
- define pilot success metrics and criteria
- prepare sales conversations and outreach assets
- capture launch feedback into a learning loop
```

## Gated Work

```text
- public health or performance claims without evidence
- wellness positioning drift
- athlete-facing messaging without proof review
- launch activity before pilot proof and approval
- pricing or commercial commitments without stakeholder signoff
```
