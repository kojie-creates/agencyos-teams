## Who this is for

Riders who race or train in **heat + duration + helmet + hands-on-bars**: motocross, SCORE/Baja two-wheel, hard enduro, GNCC, serious MTB / adventure moto.

Not: marathoners, team-sport sidelines, wellness buyers, "all elite athletes."

---

## Positioning statement

FluidLogic GPR50 is a **race-built intelligent hydration system** for two-wheel athletes who cannot take a hand off the controls to drink.

It is not a CamelBak with an app. It is not a sports drink. It is not a lifestyle bottle.

---

## Category

**Performance infrastructure** — timed, in-helmet water delivery — not hydration content, not electrolytes (water-only; other fluids can void warranty).

---

## Before → after (operational, not medical)

| Before | After (what the system does — do not promise outcomes) |
| --- | --- |
| Bite a hose, fumble a bottle, or wait for a pit | Button on bar/clutch doses water in-helmet |

Do not add "therefore you finish faster / think clearer."

## Narrative (short)

Baja and two-wheel racing taught a design rule: water that is *available* is not the same as water you can take **without breaking grip or attention**. GPR50 is that system on the rider — helmet boom, bar remote, 50oz pack — at $650, water only.

Motorsports cockpit (VMR) stays the **proof climate**, not the Segment A SKU. Do not pitch VMR to motocross. Do not pitch GPR50 as the NASCAR system.

---

## Pillars

1. **Hands stay on the controls** — remote on bar or clutch; in-helmet delivery. Rider reviews already say this (testimony, not science).
2. **Built in the same heat/duration problem as racing** — company origin in Baja; GPR50 used in SCORE / hard enduro / GNCC (company-stated). Translate, don't bury.
3. **Dosing, not sipping** — microdose spec is manufacturer-stated; app plan is an *estimate*.
4. **Premium system, not a mix** — $650 hardware. Compare to a race radio or a good helmet, not to LMNT.
