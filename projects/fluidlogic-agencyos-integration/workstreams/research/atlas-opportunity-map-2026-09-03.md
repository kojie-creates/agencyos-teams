## What transfers (and what does not)

FluidLogic sells **systems**, not drinks.

| Product | What it actually is | Implied user constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VMR** | Vehicle-mounted pump, steering-wheel drink button, MagLock helmet interface, programmable intervals | User is in a **vehicle / cockpit**. Not a runner. |
| **GPR50** | $650 wearable pack, 50oz, in-helmet boom, handlebar/clutch remote, haptics, app sweat-loss *estimate*, water only | User has **handlebars or a clutch** and usually a helmet. Filled weight ~2.6 kg. Sports drinks void warranty. |

**Transfer thesis (hypothesis, not fact):** the valuable idea is *low-distraction, timed dosing under heat + cognitive load*. The current SKUs are built for **hands-on-controls + helmet**. Elite sports without that kit need a different product or a dishonest fit.

BikeMag (public) quotes CEO Sara Blackmer that GPR50 uses the same foundational tech as pro motorsports and that later versions were planned for trail running and hiking. That is a **company expansion intent**, not a current GPR50 fit.

## Segments (ranked by proof transfer, not TAM)

### A. Motorsports-adjacent two-wheel (highest transfer)

- **Who:** motocross, SCORE/Baja two-wheel, hard enduro, GNCC, serious MTB / adventure moto
- **Pain:** hands must stay on bars; hose fumbling; heat; long stints
- **Buyer:** athlete, team manager, sponsor
- **Influencer:** other racers, series peers
- **Trigger:** race weekend, heat, prior DNF/dehydration story
- **Proof they need:** "I can drink without taking a hand off" — already in GPR50 reviews (Preston B., Scotty C., May 2026, fluidlogic.com)
- **Objection:** price ($650), weight, water-only, helmet fit, "CamelBak is enough"
- **Pilot angle:** 10–15 GPR50 units with riders who already race in heat with full-face/open-face helmets. Success = they drink on-course without breaking grip and would race again with it.
- **Why first:** product-market fit already exists. This is **expansion of a working wedge**, not a new category.

### B. Four-wheel / cockpit athletes (high transfer, different SKU)

- **Who:** NASCAR, IndyCar, IMSA, off-road four-wheel, maybe rally
- **Pain:** cockpit heat, can't manage a bottle, long stints
- **Buyer:** team / crew chief / performance staff (committee)
- **Proof:** VMR already sold into these series (product page + 2026 editorial naming Reddick, Palou, Arciero/Moore)
- **Objection:** team integration, electrical, series rules, "we already have a drink button"
- **Pilot angle:** **not** the elite-athlete *new market* test. This is core business. Do not confuse "more Cup teams" with "elite athlete transition."
- **Atlas note:** keep this lane funded as core. Do not use it as the *new* beachhead story.

### C. Heat-heavy endurance *without* bars (medium-low transfer today)

- **Who:** long-course triathlon, ultra, heat-adapted road cycling *if* a bar remote still works, maybe heat-camp endurance
- **Pain:** timed drinking, gut issues, forgetting to drink
- **Buyer:** athlete + coach
- **Gap:** GPR50 is helmet + handlebar + 2.6 kg pack + water only. Running / swimming / track is a **product gap**. Cycling is the only near-fit.
- **Objection:** "that's a moto pack"; UCI/gear rules; electrolytes needed (warranty conflict)
- **Pilot angle:** **only** if Juan wants a *new* SKU conversation. A heat-camp **cycling** cell (handlebars exist) is the least dishonest test. Do not strap GPR50 on marathoners and call it GTM.
- **Prior Aug 18 wedge #1 (tri/ultra/run) is too broad** until a wearable exists that is not helmet+bar.

### D. Team-sport performance staff (low transfer today)

- **Who:** football, soccer, tennis, combat — via trainer / performance director
- **Pain:** session hydration compliance, heat practices
- **Buyer:** performance director (slow committee)
- **Gap:** no vehicle, often no helmet/bars, electrolytes common, sideline bottles already exist
- **Nix / Epicore** already occupy *measurement*. FluidLogic is *delivery*. Teams may want data, not a $650 pack.
- **Pilot angle:** defer. High proof burden, wrong kit.

### E. Tactical / first responder (adjacent, not "elite athlete")

- **Who:** military / first responder (site already offers discount)
- **Pain:** hands-busy, heat, duration
- **Buyer:** unit / procurement
- **Note:** real, but it is **not** the brief. Do not mix into athlete GTM.

### F. Wellness / lifestyle (excluded)

Everyday hydration, "feel your best," biohacking, generic recovery. Atlas will not research this lane.
