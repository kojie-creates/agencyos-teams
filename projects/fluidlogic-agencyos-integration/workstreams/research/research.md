# Research Workstream

## Goal

```text
Identify the best athlete segments, buying paths, and wedge opportunities that can credibly leverage FluidLogic's motorsports and tactical proof without drifting into generic wellness.
```

## Source-backed findings

```text
- FluidLogic publicly frames Intelligent Hydration™ as personalized, data-informed hydration for athlete or vehicle performance.
- The company’s Baja 1000 article emphasizes heat, fatigue, and decision-making under sustained exertion.
- Nix Pro monitors fluid and electrolyte losses in real time for administrators and coaches.
- Epicore’s Gx Sweat Patch is positioned as a sweat-monitoring technology in high-performance settings.
- LMNT and Skratch occupy consumer hydration and electrolyte categories rather than integrated delivery systems.
```

## Research conclusion

```text
The strongest opportunity is not a wellness hydration storyline. It is a premium performance-system lane in which FluidLogic can stand for data-informed hydration under heat and load.
```

## Most credible wedge

```text
High-heat endurance and high-load performance athletes with a coach or performance team involved.
```

## Buyer map

```text
- athlete
- coach
- trainer
- team performance staff
- sponsor or manager
```

## Deliverable

```text
Opportunity map with wedge ranking, buyer map, objections, and proof requirements. The initial analysis indicates that the gap is in integrated delivery, not in generic hydration education.
```
