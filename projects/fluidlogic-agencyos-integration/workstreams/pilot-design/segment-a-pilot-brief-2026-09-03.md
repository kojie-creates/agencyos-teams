# Segment A pilot brief — GPR50 two-wheel heat

**Being:** Pax Trial method  
**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Working beachhead:** Kojie, 2026-09-03  
**SKU:** GPR50 only (not VMR)  
**Size:** 10–20 riders  
**Length:** 4–6 weeks or one race block, whichever is shorter  
**Owner:** Juan (commercial) + one operator

---

## Thesis

If we put GPR50 on riders who **already** race two-wheel in heat with a helmet and bars, they will (a) use the bar button on course, (b) say they would race with it again, and (c) give us objections we can use — without claiming they got faster.

If they will not wear 2.6 kg wet, will not accept water-only, or never press the button, **stop or revise**. Do not expand to tri/run.

---

## Who is in

**Include**
- Rides moto, hard enduro, SCORE/Baja two-wheel, GNCC, or serious MTB/adventure moto
- Uses a helmet (open or full face)
- Has handlebars or a clutch
- Will do at least one heat session or race during the window
- Can run **water only**
- Will give a weekly 10-minute check-in

**Exclude**
- No-bar sports (run, most team sports)
- Wants mix/electrolytes in the bladder
- Needs a medical or performance-outcome promise
- Can't install helmet boom / won't wear the pack
- Pure lifestyle / wellness interest

---

## What we give them

- GPR50 kit ($650 list; decide loan vs purchase vs 30-day trial — Juan)
- Install help (helmet boom + remote)
- Water-only rule in writing
- One-pager from Theo (after this brief) using Vera-approved language only
- Weekly check-in (see questions)

We do **not** give a training plan, a "hydration protocol" that implies clinical backing, or driver-celebrity decks.

---

## Success (need 2 of 3 to **continue**)

1. ≥70% of completers used the bar/clutch button during a session (self-report + any app session log they share).  
2. ≥50% say they would use it at their next race.  
3. ≥3 concrete objections or fit issues written down (this is a *learning* success).

## Failure (any one = **revise or stop**)

- Majority never install the helmet kit  
- Water-only is a deal-breaker for the segment  
- Weight/heat-on-back is a deal-breaker  
- They describe it as "a fancy CamelBak I wouldn't pay $650 for" and no one refers a peer  
- Anyone wants to publish performance-gain claims from this n

---

## Feedback questions (weekly)

1. Did you ride in heat this week? How long?  
2. Did the remote stay on the bar/clutch?  
3. About how many on-course doses? Did you still use a hose/bottle?  
4. Any dropouts: helmet fit, leaks, battery, pack heat, weight?  
5. Would you race with it this weekend — yes / no / only if X?  
6. What did you tell another rider, in their words?  
7. Anything we must not claim?

No "did you feel optimized."

---

## Decision rules

- **Continue:** success criteria met → Theo/Miles can widen *the same segment* (more two-wheel heat), still no paid blast.  
- **Revise:** interest but install/water/weight friction → change onboarding or SKU notes; do not change the sport.  
- **Stop:** segment rejects the kit → do not pivot this pilot to runners. That is a new product, new brief.

---

## Claim lock for the pilot itself

Blocked: faster laps, safer riding, NASCAR-win halo, "clinically proven," mix in the pack.
