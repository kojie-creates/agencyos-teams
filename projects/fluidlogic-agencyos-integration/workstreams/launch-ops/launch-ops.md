# Launch Operations Workstream

## Goal

```text
Turn approved positioning and pilot proof into a controlled launch rhythm that creates traction without overextending the small team.
```

## Launch Principles

```text
- keep the number of active channels small
- align outreach to the winning wedge
- prioritize proof-backed conversations
- use a clear follow-up cadence
- track what is working before scaling
```

## Working launch frame

```text
The launch should begin only after a narrow wedge is validated and the positioning system is source-bound.
```

## Deliverable

```text
Launch calendar, engagement plan, and feedback capture system focused on a single wedge, premium positioning, and structured proof-based outreach.
```
