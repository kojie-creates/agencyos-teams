# Assigned Agencies

## Agency Map

| Agency | Role | Key Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central Orchestrator | Routes the work and keeps sequence disciplined. | operating plan, handoff checklist |
| Research Agency | Maps competitive landscape, athlete need, and wedge options. | opportunity map, evidence gaps |
| Strategy & Positioning Agency | Converts proof into positioning and messaging architecture. | positioning framework, language system |
| Offer & Pilot Design Agency | Shapes the first commercial test. | pilot brief, success criteria |
| Sales Enablement Agency | Builds objection handling and sales assets. | outreach script, proof pack |
| Content Studio Agency | Produces premium marketing content. | launch assets, proof-backed narratives |
| Launch Agency | Runs activation and engagement sequence. | launch calendar, community touchpoints |
| Risk & Proof Agency | Reviews claims and safeguards public messaging. | evidence ledger, approval gates |
| Insights Agency | Tracks feedback and improves the system. | learning loop, pivot notes |

## Working Rule

```text
Central Orchestrator keeps the project moving; specialists produce, but the human remains accountable for final approval and market-facing decisions.
```
