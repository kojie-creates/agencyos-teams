# Handoffs

## Operating Sequence

```text
1. Team Lead receives brief and routes intake.
2. Central Orchestrator classifies the work and creates the routing packet.
3. Research Agency produces the landscape, athlete-science signal, and wedge options.
4. Positioning Agency turns research into a premium athlete narrative with proof-bound messages.
5. Offer & Pilot Design builds the first commercial test with clear KPI criteria.
6. Sales and Content agencies turn the strategy into managed outreach and premium assets.
7. Launch team activates the sequence with tracked engagement and customer response.
8. Insights loop converts feedback into revisions, proof needs, and market-learning updates.
```

## Required Handoff Packet

```text
- brief summary
- verified facts
- assumptions and unknowns
- deliverable summary
- approval needed
- evidence source or validation status
```

## Escalation Rule

```text
If a specialist cannot progress without guessing, it should stop and return a failure packet with the blockers and recommended recovery.
```
