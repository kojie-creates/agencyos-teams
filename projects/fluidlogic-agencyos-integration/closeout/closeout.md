# Closeout Criteria

## Definition Of Done

```text
The project is complete when the workflow, research outputs, positioning guardrails, pilot plan, launch sequence, and learning loop are documented and approved by the human decision owner.
```

## Closeout Set

```text
- project brief approved
- market wedge options ranked and reviewed
- evidence ledger updated
- pilot concept ready for next-stage approval
- launch and learning loop defined
```

## Exit Condition

```text
The human can continue with a specific next action: research expansion, positioning draft, pilot design, or launch activation.
```
