# Project Brief

## Project Name

```text
FluidLogic AgencyOS Integration
```

## Owner

```text
Juan Benitez and FluidLogic commercial leadership
```

## Outcome

```text
Create a working AgencyOS model for FluidLogic's elite athlete market transition: research-led positioning, evidence-bound claims, pilot design, sales enablement, content workflow, launch activation, and ongoing learning.
```

## Why This Needs Teams

```text
The project spans research, market segmentation, content strategy, sales enablement, evidence review, pilot design, and launch learning. A single assistant cannot safely carry the full workflow while preserving claims quality, message discipline, and handoff accountability.
```

## Value Hypothesis

```text
AgencyOS Teams can help FluidLogic preserve motorsports credibility, translate it into elite athlete language, reduce founder over-dependence on research and communications, and establish a repeatable workflow for market testing and learning.
```

## Success Criteria

```text
- Clear sequencing of agency work and handoffs
- Evidence-based positioning guardrails
- Ranked athlete market wedges
- Strong pilot design with measurable traction criteria
- Launch and learning loop that improves input over time
- No generic wellness dilution
```

## Non-Goals

```text
Do not broaden into mass-market wellness hydration.
Do not make unverified performance claims.
Do not skip evidence review before launch.
Do not treat motorsports credibility as a slogan without translation.
```
