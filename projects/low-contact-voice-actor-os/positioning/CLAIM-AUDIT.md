## Claim Rules

- Use exact evidence-backed language.
- Keep the October 2023 project private, including associated names and supporting records; possible NDA reported by actor.
- Do not mention accolades.
- Do not mention audiobook narrator credits until attribution is verified. A Spotify listing does not prove Audible availability.
- Approved turnaround wording: "Typical turnaround is 24 hours after receipt of scope for smaller projects. Timing depends on project size and is confirmed after scope review." This is an actor-supplied expectation, not a measured historical average.
- Do not imply agency representation unless true.
- Do not imply availability until actor confirms.
- Do not include AI/TTS rights in ordinary voice-over offers.
