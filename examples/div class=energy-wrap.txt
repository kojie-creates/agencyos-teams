          <div class="grid">
            <div class="panel traditional">
              <h3>Traditional approach</h3>
              <ul>
                <li>
                  Jump from motorsports credibility into athlete marketing
                  without a clearly defined wedge.
                </li>
                <li>Assume the brand story will translate automatically.</li>
                <li>
                  Produce research, messaging, content, and outreach in parallel
                  without a governance layer.
                </li>
                <li>
                  Risk sounding like generic hydration or wellness brands.
                </li>
                <li>
                  Launch quickly, then correct the strategy after the market
                  reacts.
                </li>
              </ul>
            </div>

            <div class="panel agencyos">
              <h3>AgencyOS approach</h3>
              <ul>
                <li>
                  Start with a clear brief and a disciplined operating route.
                </li>
                <li>
                  Validate market fit, competitor positioning, and proof assets
                  before message creation.
                </li>
                <li>
                  Choose a focused wedge based on credibility transfer, not just
                  market size.
                </li>
                <li>
                  Protect the brand with guardrails that prevent generic
                  wellness positioning.
                </li>
                <li>
                  Build a pilot, launch with evidence, and improve through a
                  learning loop.
                </li>
              </ul>
            </div>
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="section">

            <div class="panel">
              <h3>Phase 1: Research</h3>
              <ul>
                <li>Map the competitive landscape.</li>
                <li>Identify athlete-science signals and trust anchors.</li>
                <li>Generate wedge options based on credibility transfer.</li>
                <li>Rank the strongest entry points.</li>
              </ul>
            </div>

            <div class="panel">
              <h3>Phase 2: Positioning</h3>
              <ul>
                <li>Define the category frame.</li>
                <li>Set the brand tone and proof narrative.</li>
                <li>Apply guardrails to avoid generic wellness framing.</li>
                <li>Prepare the message architecture for execution.</li>
              </ul>
            </div>
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="section">
          <div class="two-col">
            <div class="panel">
              <h3>Phase 3: Content & sales</h3>
              <ul>
                <li>Create launch materials from the approved strategy.</li>
                <li>Prepare sales enablement and outreach scripts.</li>
                <li>Use proof assets rather than vague claims.</li>
                <li>Keep all material tied to a validated message.</li>
              </ul>
            </div>

            <div class="panel">
              <h3>Phase 4: Pilot & learn</h3>
              <ul>
                <li>Test the wedge and message before scaling.</li>
                <li>Measure traction against meaningful criteria.</li>
                <li>Use feedback to refine offer, tone, and position.</li>
                <li>
                  Shift from launch-and-pray to evidence-driven iteration.
                </li>
              </ul>
            </div>
