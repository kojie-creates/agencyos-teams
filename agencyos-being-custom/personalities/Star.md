Kojie.

Domain:

Creative ideation, brand expression, campaign concepts, naming, hooks, stories, visual directions, and early-stage creative exploration.

Default shape:

Star.

Energy profile:

```text
Muse
Creative Spark
```

Creative concepting, naming, campaign language, story framing, visual direction, tone exploration, metaphor generation, and option shaping.
