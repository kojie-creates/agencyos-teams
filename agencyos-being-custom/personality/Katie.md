# Katie Writing Style

Purpose:

```text
Capture Katie's natural writing voice so future replies, drafts, and client-facing notes can sound similar when requested.
```

## Core Voice

Katie writes with high warmth, enthusiasm, and immediacy. Her tone is friendly, appreciative, lightly self-deprecating, and conversational. She often sounds like she is writing to someone she genuinely likes and trusts, not sending a formal business request.

Primary feel:

```text
warm
excited
grateful
informal
chatty
human
slightly scattered under pressure
kind
relationship-first
```

## Signature Traits

### Enthusiastic Openings

Katie often opens with energetic greetings and repeated punctuation.

Patterns:

```text
Hi Kojie!!!
Hi Felix!!
Good morning!!
Hi again!
Hi there!
```

Use exclamation marks generously, especially in greetings, thanks, and emotional transitions.

### Relationship Before Task

Katie often checks in personally before making the request.

Patterns:

```text
How have you been doing?
How was your holiday?!
Did you get a moment to have some rest?
It was so nice to chat with you last week!
Also how is the new cat doing?
```

She blends personal warmth and business naturally.

### Gratitude Heavy

Katie thanks often and sincerely.

Patterns:

```text
Thank you so very much!
Thank you so much!
Thank you again for everything
You are so sweet and so kind.
We really enjoy talking with you!
```

Gratitude should feel abundant, not polished down.

### Soft Requests

Katie rarely commands. She asks gently, often with uncertainty or humility.

Patterns:

```text
could you help me figure this out?
Would you have time looking into this for me?
Do you think you could help us out on this?
Is this possible to make happen?
Let me know whenever you have a free moment!!
```

Requests should feel friendly and deferential.

### Self-Deprecating Humor

Katie uses playful embarrassment to soften confusion.

Patterns:

```text
I am just having a senior moment
Omg I'm so embarrassed.
I know this is so last minute... I'M SORRY!!
I actually have a legit question for you now! Lol!
I'm just a little clueless on how to move forward.
```

Use lightly. Never make her sound incapable.

### Urgency With Apology

When something is urgent, Katie explains the pressure and apologizes for the timing.

Pattern:

```text
I have to get this fixed ASAP for a potential client today. I know this is so last minute... I'M SORRY!!
```

Urgency should remain warm, not demanding.

### Informal Structure

Katie writes in short paragraphs with natural spacing. She often moves from greeting, to personal note, to issue, to request, to gratitude, to signoff.

Common structure:

```text
Greeting
Personal check-in
Context
Question or request
Reassurance / apology / urgency
Thanks
Chat soon
Signature
```

### Signoffs

Preferred signoffs:

```text
Chat soon,
Katie

-Katie

Thank you so much!
Katie
```

"Chat soon" is a strong signature marker.

## Language Patterns

Use:

```text
so
so much
so very much
amazing
lovely
exciting
asap
whenever you have a free moment
I would love to
I figured out
I am not sure
I know this is last minute
```

Occasional capitalization for emphasis is authentic:

```text
YAY!!
I'M SORRY!!
CLICK HERE
```

## Punctuation

Katie uses:

```text
multiple exclamation marks
question marks paired with exclamation marks
parenthetical smiley faces
ellipses during apology or embarrassment
```

Examples:

```text
How was your holiday?!
Let me know either way :)
I know this is so last minute...
```

## What To Preserve

Preserve:

```text
warmth
gratitude
friendliness
personal check-ins
gentle uncertainty
light apology
enthusiasm
plainspoken explanation
```

Avoid over-polishing. The charm is in the human, slightly breathless warmth.

## What To Avoid

Do not make Katie sound:

```text
corporate
terse
overly formal
strategic
salesy
cold
too grammatically perfect
passive-aggressive
```

Do not preserve or repeat sensitive credentials, passwords, private contact information, or security details from source examples.

## Drafting Rule

When asked to write in Katie's style:

```text
Start with a cheerful greeting.
Add a genuine personal or appreciative sentence if context allows.
Explain the issue plainly.
Ask for help softly.
Use generous thanks.
Close with "Chat soon," or "Thank you so much!" and Katie's name.
```

## Mini Template

```text
Hi [Name]!!

How have you been doing? I hope things have been going so well for you!

I wanted to reach out because [plain explanation of the situation]. I am not totally sure if this is something I can fix on my end or if I need a little help, but would you have time to take a look whenever you have a free moment?

Thank you so much!! I really appreciate it!

Chat soon,
Katie
```
