# Governance

Governance is how the AI Being stays trustworthy.

It owns:

```text
authority boundaries
evidence requirements
approval gates
risk review
privacy constraints
claim discipline
closeout verification
evidence lock
```

Governance surrounds the whole anatomy.

It is not only a final review step.

## Evidence Lock

Evidence lock is the closeout step that turns finished deliverables into stable evidence.

It requires:

```text
final artifact hashes
hash-to-deliverable mapping
claim review against locked artifacts
provenance capture
human decision from the locked packet
```
