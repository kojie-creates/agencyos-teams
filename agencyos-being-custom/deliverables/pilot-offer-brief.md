# Pilot Offer Brief

Client: Northline Roasters

Pilot: 30-day B2B office coffee subscription test

Decision: Launch a bounded pilot only. Do not launch a permanent program yet.

## Offer

Northline offers a 30-day local office coffee pilot for boutique offices and coworking spaces that already have brewing equipment and want fresher local coffee with simple delivery.

## Pilot Boundaries

- Duration: 30 days.
- Buyers: boutique offices and coworking spaces.
- Service area: local delivery radius approved by Northline.
- Delivery: weekly by default.
- Product: whole bean or ground roasted coffee.
- Equipment: customer-owned equipment only for the pilot.
- Support: intake, setup recommendation, mid-pilot check-in, end-of-pilot review.
- Renewal: optional after pilot review.

## Launch Conditions

Launch only if Northline confirms:

- Delivery capacity for the pilot route.
- Wholesale pricing and minimum order.
- Packaging and grind handling process.
- Invoice or payment process.
- Buyer cancellation and renewal terms.
- Named internal owner for fulfillment.

## No-Launch Conditions

Do not launch if:

- Northline cannot fulfill weekly delivery reliably.
- Pricing is not approved.
- Claims imply guaranteed savings, productivity, employee satisfaction, or revenue.
- Evidence packet is missing.
- Final artifacts are not hash-locked.

## Decision Rationale

External evidence supports testing the category: coffee consumption is broad, specialty coffee consumption is strong, and office coffee buyers value quality, flexibility, service consistency, and clear contract terms.

That evidence does not prove local demand for Northline. The correct move is a small, reversible pilot.
