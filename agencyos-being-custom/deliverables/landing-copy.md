# Northline Roasters Office Coffee Pilot

## Hero

Better office coffee, delivered locally for 30 days.

Northline Roasters is testing a small wholesale subscription pilot for boutique offices and coworking spaces that want fresh, locally roasted coffee without a long contract.

## Offer

Join the 30-day Northline Office Coffee Pilot.

You get:

- Weekly delivery of whole bean or ground coffee.
- A simple office intake before launch.
- Roast recommendations matched to brew setup and team size.
- One mid-pilot check-in.
- Month-end usage review.
- Clear renewal, pause, or stop options.

## Who It Fits

Best for offices and coworking teams that:

- Serve coffee to staff, guests, members, or meeting rooms.
- Already own basic brewing equipment.
- Want better coffee without changing the whole office pantry system.
- Prefer a local roaster relationship over a large generic vendor.
- Can give feedback during the pilot.

## What Is Proven

Coffee demand is broad, and recent industry reporting shows specialty coffee has strong consumer adoption.

## What Is Assumed

Northline still needs pilot evidence on local office buyer demand, delivery workload, repeat usage, reorder behavior, and acceptable price points.

## Call To Action

Apply for the 30-day office coffee pilot.

No long contract. No guaranteed savings claim. No revenue promise. Just a practical test of fit.
