# Northline Pilot Run Example

Status: simulated example

Decision owner: Kojie

Operating rule: treat this as a practice run, not evidence of real market demand.

## Confirmed Pilot Setup

- Pilot length: 30 days.
- Service area: local delivery only.
- Fulfillment owner: Northline operations lead.
- Delivery cadence: weekly.
- Account cap: 3 pilot accounts.
- Product scope: coffee only.
- Equipment scope: customer-owned equipment only.
- Claims: no savings, revenue, productivity, health, or satisfaction guarantees.

## Week 1

- Deliver first orders.
- Confirm grind and storage.
- Record baseline usage.
- Ask buyer to report early issues only.

Result:

- Riverdesk requests slightly finer grind.
- Finch & Row reports good setup fit.

## Week 2

- Adjust Riverdesk grind.
- Keep Finch & Row unchanged.
- Check reorder pace.

Result:

- Riverdesk usage is steady.
- Finch & Row asks about adding a decaf option.

## Week 3

- Add optional decaf quote for Finch & Row.
- Confirm delivery time windows.
- Check for service problems.

Result:

- No missed deliveries.
- One account asks for clearer invoice line items.

## Week 4

- Complete final review.
- Ask renew, revise, pause, or stop.
- Capture buyer feedback.

Result:

- Riverdesk: renew if pricing remains simple.
- Finch & Row: renew with optional decaf.
- Studio Wren remains waitlisted.

## Example Decision

Continue for a second 30-day operating test with the same account cap unless Northline confirms more capacity.

Do not announce a public wholesale subscription program yet.

## Why

The example run shows operational promise, but only with a tiny sample. It still does not prove durable demand, margin, delivery capacity at scale, or repeatable acquisition.
