# Target Customer Profile

## Primary Buyer

Boutique office manager, coworking community manager, founder-operator, people operations lead, or executive assistant responsible for workplace experience.

## Best-Fit Account

- Local office or coworking space.
- Small enough to value a direct vendor relationship.
- Large enough to consume coffee weekly.
- Existing brewer, grinder, or pantry setup.
- Dissatisfied with generic coffee, stale beans, unclear service, or rigid vendor terms.
- Willing to give pilot feedback.

## Buying Triggers

- Office reopening or refresh.
- Coworking member experience upgrade.
- Pantry vendor review.
- Client meeting experience improvement.
- Local vendor preference.
- Complaint about current coffee quality.

## Disqualifiers

- Requires national account coverage.
- Requires equipment leasing during this pilot.
- Requires guaranteed cost savings.
- Requires daily emergency support.
- Has no buyer or approver.
- Cannot receive delivery within Northline's practical route.

## Proof Needed During Pilot

- Number of qualified inquiries.
- Sample-to-pilot conversion.
- Weekly usage consistency.
- Delivery effort per account.
- Buyer feedback.
- Renewal interest.
- Operational issues.
