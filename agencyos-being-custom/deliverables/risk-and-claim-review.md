# Risk And Claim Review

Risk level: commercial and public-facing claim risk.

## Allowed Claims

- Northline is an independent roastery if verified by Northline.
- The pilot is 30 days.
- The pilot is for boutique offices and coworking spaces.
- The pilot uses weekly delivery if Northline approves fulfillment capacity.
- The pilot is local if delivery radius is defined.
- Coffee is roasted by Northline if operationally true.

## Blocked Claims

- Guaranteed cost savings.
- Guaranteed revenue.
- Guaranteed employee satisfaction.
- Productivity improvement.
- Health benefit.
- Sustainability leadership unless separately evidenced.
- Best coffee, highest quality, or category superiority.
- Guaranteed freshness unless roast and delivery timing is specified and met.

## Evidence Gaps

- Northline's exact delivery radius.
- Northline's wholesale pricing.
- Northline's production capacity.
- Current B2B buyer demand in Northline's market.
- Exact office account economics.
- Packaging costs and delivery labor.

## Required Approval Before Public Use

Kojie must approve the final locked packet.

Northline must approve:

- pricing,
- delivery area,
- service terms,
- brand copy,
- launch timing.
