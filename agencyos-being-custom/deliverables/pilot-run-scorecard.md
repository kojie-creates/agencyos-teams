# Pilot Run Scorecard

Status: simulated example

## Decision

Continue testing. Do not scale yet.

## Pass Signals

- Qualified buyers could be identified.
- The offer stayed understandable.
- Coffee-only scope reduced operational complexity.
- Weekly delivery worked in the example run.
- Buyer feedback produced practical adjustments.

## Caution Signals

- One buyer wanted equipment included.
- One buyer requested decaf expansion.
- Invoice clarity became an operational issue.
- Account count stayed too small for demand proof.
- No margin validation was performed.

## Next Operating Test

Run one more 30-day test with:

- same account cap,
- clearer invoice template,
- optional decaf add-on,
- written delivery windows,
- explicit renewal decision form.

## Do Not Do Yet

- Do not increase account cap.
- Do not promise equipment service.
- Do not publish performance claims.
- Do not call this proven demand.
- Do not create a long-term subscription page without updated approval.
