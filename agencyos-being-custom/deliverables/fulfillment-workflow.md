# Fulfillment Workflow

## Owner

Northline assigns one pilot owner before outreach starts.

## Workflow

1. Receive intake.
2. Confirm office fit and delivery route.
3. Estimate weekly coffee quantity.
4. Recommend roast, grind, and delivery cadence.
5. Send pilot quote and terms.
6. Receive approval.
7. Schedule first roast and delivery.
8. Pack order with account label.
9. Deliver on agreed day.
10. Record delivery completion.
11. Send mid-pilot check-in.
12. Adjust quantity or grind if needed.
13. Complete final pilot review.
14. Decide renew, revise, pause, or stop.

## Required Records

- Intake response.
- Quote and accepted terms.
- Delivery dates.
- Coffee quantity.
- Grind setting.
- Issues and resolutions.
- Buyer feedback.
- Renewal decision.

## Controls

- No equipment commitment in the first pilot.
- No unapproved delivery radius expansion.
- No recurring subscription after day 30 without buyer approval.
- No public claims from pilot feedback without permission and evidence.
