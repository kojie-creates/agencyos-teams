# Sales Call Script

## Open

Thanks for taking the time. The goal is not to sell you a big program today. We are checking fit for a 30-day office coffee pilot.

## Fit Questions

1. How does coffee work in the office today?
2. Who drinks it: staff, members, guests, meeting rooms, or all of those?
3. What equipment do you already have?
4. What is not working with the current setup?
5. How often do you restock?
6. Who approves a small pilot?
7. What would make the pilot worth continuing after 30 days?

## Positioning

Northline can provide locally roasted coffee on a simple weekly delivery schedule. For the pilot, we are keeping it narrow: coffee supply, setup matching, check-in, and renewal decision.

## Claim Guardrails

Do say:

- We can test whether this fits your office.
- We can match coffee to your current setup.
- We can keep the pilot short and reversible.

Do not say:

- This will save money.
- This will improve productivity.
- Employees will be happier.
- This will replace every office coffee vendor.

## Close

Based on what you shared, I think this is either:

- a fit for the 30-day pilot,
- worth revisiting later,
- or not a fit because the scope is outside this pilot.

If it is a fit, the next step is the intake form and a pilot quote.
