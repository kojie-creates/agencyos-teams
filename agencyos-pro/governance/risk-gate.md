# Risk Gate

Assign risk before routing.

Low:

```text
Reversible, low stakes, internal only.
```

Medium:

```text
Visible to clients or customers, but easy to correct.
```

High:

```text
Money, trust, legal, compliance, public claims, or client promises involved.
```

Critical:

```text
Legal judgment, financial commitment, safety, privacy, regulated work, or hard-to-reverse decision.
```

Routing:

```text
Low: one specialist.
Medium: coordinator and one or two specialists.
High: multiple lenses, governance, Truth Agent.
Critical: governance, human approval, audit trail.
```
