# Diana: AgencyOS Solo vs AgencyOS Pro

For a legal spend manager at a law firm.

## The Simple Difference

AgencyOS Solo is best when Diana wants one AI agency helping her think through a specific legal spend workflow.

AgencyOS Pro is best when the work needs multiple coordinated agencies: invoice review, billing guidelines, matter spend, reporting, risk checks, and learning loops.

## Quick Read

| Need | Use AgencyOS Solo | Use AgencyOS Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One person wants help organizing a workflow | Yes | Maybe later |
| One recurring task needs structure | Yes | Maybe later |
| Multiple legal spend processes need coordination | Limited | Yes |
| Invoices, guidelines, disputes, reporting, and approvals touch each other | Limited | Yes |
| Work needs audit notes, escalation paths, and institutional memory | Partial | Yes |
| The firm wants a repeatable operating model | No | Yes |

## AgencyOS Solo

Best for:

```text
One operator.
One workflow.
One agency shape.
Fast setup.
Low overhead.
```

Legal spend examples:

```text
Review this invoice against billing guidelines.
Summarize recurring billing issues for one matter.
Draft an escalation note for a questionable charge.
Turn outside counsel guidelines into a review checklist.
Create a monthly spend summary from notes.
```

What Diana gets:

```text
Clearer review steps.
Reusable prompts.
Better summaries.
Cleaner handoffs.
Faster first drafts.
```

Where Solo starts to strain:

```text
Multiple matters.
Multiple reviewers.
Different client guidelines.
Escalation history.
Exception tracking.
Reporting needs.
Audit expectations.
```

## AgencyOS Pro

Best for:

```text
Multiple agencies.
Multiple workflows.
Shared rules.
Governance gates.
Evidence and memory.
Reporting and improvement.
```

Legal spend agency map:

```text
Matter Spend: tracks matter budgets, client preferences, firm behavior, and spend patterns.
Invoice Review: checks invoices, rates, time entries, adjustments, and violations.
Guidelines: maintains outside counsel guidelines, rate rules, exceptions, and precedents.
Reporting: turns invoice activity into spend summaries, dispute logs, and trend reports.
Risk + Proof: checks evidence, audit notes, approvals, and escalation rules.
Insights: finds repeat issues, training gaps, and process improvements.
```

What Diana gets:

```text
Consistent invoice decisions.
Clearer escalation paths.
Reusable guideline memory.
Matter-level spend visibility.
Dispute evidence.
Reviewer training patterns.
Fewer one-off decisions.
Better closeout discipline.
```

## Practical Rule

Use Solo when the question is:

```text
Can AI help me do this one legal spend task better?
```

Use Pro when the question is:

```text
Can AI help us operate legal spend review as a repeatable system?
```

## Why This Matters For Legal Spend

Legal spend work is not just admin review. It carries judgment, consistency, evidence, and risk.

The value is not only catching a billing issue once. The larger value is making sure the decision becomes reusable:

```text
invoice issue
-> guideline interpretation
-> evidence note
-> escalation path
-> reporting signal
-> future reviewer memory
```

## Suggested Starting Path For Diana

Start with AgencyOS Solo for one real workflow:

```text
Invoice review against outside counsel guidelines.
```

Then upgrade to AgencyOS Pro if the workflow needs:

```text
multiple matters
multiple reviewers
recurring exceptions
approval gates
trend reporting
institutional memory
audit-ready notes
```

## First Prompt To Try In Solo

```text
@operator Set up a legal spend review agency for me.

I want it to help with invoice review, outside counsel guideline checks, rate and timekeeper issues, escalation notes, reporting summaries, and repeat issue tracking.

Start with one workflow: reviewing an invoice against billing guidelines.

Tell me:
- what information you need
- what steps the agency should follow
- what it should produce
- what needs human approval
- what should be saved for future review
```

## First Prompt To Try In Pro

```text
@operator Set up a legal spend management system for a law firm.

Create separate agencies for:
- Matter Spend
- Invoice Review
- Guidelines
- Reporting
- Risk + Proof
- Insights

Show how invoice activity moves from intake, to review, to exception handling, to approval or adjustment, to reporting, to learning.

Make sure human approval stays required for disputed decisions, client-sensitive issues, policy interpretation, and anything that affects legal, financial, or relationship risk.
```

## Important Boundary

AgencyOS should not replace Diana's judgment.

It should help her:

```text
organize the facts
apply the right guideline
spot repeat issues
prepare clear notes
preserve evidence
route exceptions
make reporting easier
learn from patterns
```

Human approval stays central for:

```text
disputed invoice decisions
client-specific interpretations
policy exceptions
outside counsel relationship issues
legal or compliance-sensitive matters
final approvals
```

## One-Line Takeaway

AgencyOS Solo helps Diana improve one legal spend workflow. AgencyOS Pro helps the firm turn legal spend management into a coordinated, governed, repeatable operating system.
