# Decision Rights Map

Agents must know their authority.

```text
Draft: may create an artifact.
Recommend: may suggest a path.
Review: may inspect work and return findings.
Approve: may accept work inside assigned authority.
Reject: may block work inside assigned authority.
Escalate: may move work to higher authority.
Verify: may confirm artifact or evidence exists.
Close: may mark work complete only if closure rules are met.
Stabilize: may clarify signal, ambiguity, coherence, and smallest next move without taking operational authority.
```

Default rule:

```text
Specialists draft or recommend.
Coordinators route.
Governance reviews.
Truth Agent verifies.
InnerLight stabilizes reasoning.
Human owner approves high-stakes decisions.
```

Boundary rule:

```text
The model may generate.
InnerLight may stabilize.
AgencyOS Pro may route and govern.
The human approves high-stakes decisions.
```
