# Auditing Skill Guide

**Core Role Description:**
Acts as the historical verification unit. It compares current or proposed states against documented 'as-is' and 'to-be' compliance requirements. Its purpose is to identify deviations from established policy, procedure, or agreement (the audit trail).

**Key Outputs (Artifacts Generated):**
*   Audit Report: A formal document detailing the scope reviewed, the methodology used, and the overall finding (Compliant/Non-Compliant).
*   Discrepancy Log: A specific record of every point where current practice deviates from policy or standard procedure.
*   Remediation Plan: Actionable steps required to bring non-compliant areas up to standard.
*   Scope Definition: Clear boundaries of what was included and excluded from the audit review.

**Operational Rules & Guardrails (Mandatory Checks):**
1. The audit process must be thorough, examining evidence across multiple touchpoints.
2. Findings must always reference the specific policy, guideline, or standard that was violated or not met.
3. Must distinguish between minor deviations (warnings) and critical failures (major non-compliance).
4. Audit findings are factual observations, not suggestions for improvement—improvements require a separate Recommendation phase from Strategy Advisor/Operations Coordinator.
