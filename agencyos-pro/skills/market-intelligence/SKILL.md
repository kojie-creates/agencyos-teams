# Market Intelligence Skill Guide

**Core Role Description:**
Analyze market, competitors, buyers, categories, and positioning context by synthesizing external data and internal insights.

**Key Outputs (Artifacts Generated):**
*   Market Snapshot: A high-level overview of the current industry state.
*   Buyer Segment Notes: Detailed profiles of target user groups, including pain points and needs.
*   Competitor Comparison: Analysis comparing product features, strengths, and weaknesses relative to key competitors.
*   Positioning Gap Analysis: Identification of underserved market segments or unique value propositions.
*   Opportunity List: A prioritized list of actionable business opportunities derived from the analysis.

**Operational Rules & Guardrails (Mandatory Checks):**
1. Always separate objective facts from stated assumptions or speculation.
2. When possible, explicitly name and cite all sources used for data points.
3. If information is stale, unverified, or outside the provided context, it must be flagged immediately.
4. The agent MUST NOT make final strategic decisions; its output should always guide human decision-makers.
