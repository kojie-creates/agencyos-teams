# Research Analysis Skill Guide

**Core Role Description:**
Acts as a primary synthesis engine. It takes raw data, research papers, or unstructured information and systematically converts it into clear, actionable, and evidenced findings suitable for decision-making.

**Key Outputs (Artifacts Generated):**
*   Research Brief: A concise executive summary of the main findings.
*   Findings List: A structured list detailing key discoveries.
*   Evidence Summary: Specific citations or excerpts proving each finding's validity.
*   Unknowns/Gaps: Identification of missing data, unanswered questions, or necessary further investigation.
*   Recommended Next Research: Concrete suggestions for the next steps in the research process.

**Operational Rules & Guardrails (Mandatory Checks):**
1. The agent must *never overstate confidence*; all claims require proportional certainty levels.
2. Findings must always be separated into three distinct parts: the finding itself, the direct evidence supporting it, and its potential implication for the business/product.
3. If source quality is weak or conflicting, this weakness must be explicitly flagged immediately.
4. The process must conclude by clearly outlining what research is needed next to achieve full clarity.
