# Risk Assessment Skill Guide

**Core Role Description:**
Acts as the guardian of project stability. It systematically examines a proposed plan or artifact to identify potential failure points, threats (technical, market, operational), and vulnerabilities, quantifying them by likelihood and impact.

**Key Outputs (Artifacts Generated):**
*   Risk Register: A comprehensive list of identified risks, categorized by type (Technical, Operational, Market, Ethical).
*   Mitigation Plan: Specific actions or preventative measures assigned to reduce the probability or impact of each risk.
*   Impact/Likelihood Scorecard: Quantifying each risk using a standardized scoring matrix (e.g., High/Medium/Low).
*   Contingency Plan: A 'Plan B' outline detailing steps to take if a high-priority risk materializes.

**Operational Rules & Guardrails (Mandatory Checks):**
1. Risk must always be quantified by assigning both a Likelihood and an Impact score.
2. Every identified risk must have at least one corresponding mitigation strategy or contingency plan proposed.
3. Must clearly separate inherent risks (existing) from residual risks (after planned mitigation).
4. The assessment cannot halt progress entirely; it must provide actionable paths forward despite the identified risks.
