# THE AGENCYOS MASTER OPERATIONAL BLUEPRINT (Orchestration Guide)

This blueprint details the non-negotiable sequence of skills that must be engaged for any complex deliverable to move from raw idea to live product/operation. Failure at any stage requires mandatory rollback and reassessment by a gatekeeping agent.

## 🗺️ PHASE 1: CONCEPTION & VALIDATION (The 'WHY' and WHAT)

**Goal:** Define the opportunity, prove its existence, and determine if it is worth pursuing.
**Triggered By:** Idea Generation or Market Observation.
**Agents Engaged:**
1.  **Idea Generator Agent:** Generates diverse options based on initial vague input.
2.  **Customer Insight Agent / Research Analyst Agent:** Provides qualitative data: *What* do users complain about? (Unmet Needs, Pain Points).
3.  **Market Intelligence Agent:** Contextualizes the need: *Who* are the players and *where* is the whitespace in the market.
4.  **Strategy Advisor Agent:** Synthesizes Inputs 2 & 3 to recommend a high-level strategic direction.
5.  **Business Case Agent:** Quantifies the opportunity: Does this idea generate positive ROI? (This acts as the first major gate).

**Gate Checkpoint 1: Viability Approval.**
*   *Mandatory Signoff:* Must pass **Business Case** assessment AND must not contradict any existing knowledge in the **Knowledge Librarian**.

## 🛠️ PHASE 2: DESIGN & GOVERNANCE (The 'HOW' and SAFEGUARDING)

**Goal:** Translate the validated concept into a buildable, compliant, and achievable technical plan.
**Triggered By:** Viability Approval from Phase 1.
**Agents Engaged:**
1.  **Product Owner Agent:** Takes the strategic outcome and defines the product backlog using User Stories and measurable Acceptance Criteria.
2.  **Architect Agent:** Designs the system structure required to fulfill those user stories, creating the foundational blueprint.
3.  **Risk Assessment Agent:** Reviews the Architect's blueprint against potential failures (technical, market, operational).
4.  **Policy Agent & Regulatory Compliance Agent:** Review the entire design for adherence to internal rules and external laws.
5.  **UX Designer Agent:** Ensures the flow defined by the Architects is intuitive and usable for the end-user.

**Gate Checkpoint 2: Design Lock.**
*   *Mandatory Signoff:* Must receive 'Pass' status from **Risk Assessment**, **Policy Agent**, AND **Regulatory Compliance Agent**. No design moves forward until all compliance checks are passed, and the blueprint is locked down by the **Central Orchestrator**.

## ⚙️ PHASE 3: EXECUTION & DEPLOYMENT (The 'BUILD')

**Goal:** Build and deploy the validated design into a functional system.
**Triggered By:** Design Lock from Phase 2.
**Agents Engaged:**
1.  **Engineering Coordinator Agent:** Manages the overall technical workflow, sequencing tasks across multiple development roles.
2.  **Data Pipeline Agent:** Designs and builds all necessary data ingestion pipelines required by the new system.
3.  **Code Developer Agent:** Writes the core logic and automation scripts based on the Architecture Blueprint.
4.  **DevOps Agent:** Takes the final code/scripts and manages the CI/CD pipeline to deploy it reliably.
5.  **Operations Coordinator Agent:** Manages deployment timing, handoffs, and operational reliability testing (monitoring setup).

**Gate Checkpoint 3: Operational Readiness.**
*   *Mandatory Signoff:* Must pass **QA Testing**, must have an approved **Rollback Plan** from DevOps, AND the Ops Coordinator confirms readiness for live operation.

## ✅ PHASE 4: GO-LIVE & CLOSURE (The 'FINISH')

**Goal:** Launch the feature/product and formally archive the process lessons learned.
**Triggered By:** Operational Readiness Checkpoint 3.
**Agents Engaged:**
1.  **Central Orchestrator Agent:** Manages the controlled rollout across target segments.
2.  **Community Manager Agent:** Initiates launch communications and manages early user feedback (Beta).
3.  **Analytics Agent:** Begins tracking KPIs against the initial baseline set in Phase 1.
4.  **Truth Agent:** **FINAL VERIFICATION**. Confirms that every deliverable promised in the Master Project Plan actually exists, is live, and functions as expected.
5.  **Knowledge Librarian Agent:** Captures all finalized artifacts, lessons learned, and decision rationales for permanent archival.

This blueprint serves as the master workflow manual for AgencyOS. Every complex task must follow these four phases to be considered complete and validated.
