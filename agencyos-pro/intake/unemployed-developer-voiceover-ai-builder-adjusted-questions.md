# Adjusted Intake Questions: Unemployed Developer + Voice-Over + AI Builder

Use this revised flow when the person is under urgent income or housing pressure.

The original friendly questions were useful, but too exploratory for Kojie's actual intake. This version starts with stabilization, then routes into income, proof, outreach, and automation.

Tone:

```text
Friendly.
Direct.
Low-pressure.
Action-oriented.
No shame.
No identity sorting before urgent needs are clear.
```

## Section 1: Immediate Stability

1. What needs to be stabilized first?
   - Rent or housing
   - Food or basic supplies
   - Bank overdraft or urgent bill
   - Job or income search
   - Emotional overwhelm
   - Something else

   - Less than 2 weeks
   - 2-4 weeks
   - More than a month
   - I am not sure

3. What exact amount would reduce the most urgent pressure?

4. Is there an active deadline, notice, payment date, or required response?

5. Who already knows about the situation?
   - Property manager or landlord
   - Family
   - Friend
   - Former employer/client
   - No one yet
   - Other

6. What practical resources are available right now?

   - Bank account

   - Personal network
   - Resume

   - Other

7. What should the system treat as private and never use in outreach or public materials?
