# Walkthrough: Creative Services Studio

Industry:

```text
Independent designer, copywriter, video editor, photographer, brand strategist, or small creative studio managing client work, content, revisions, proposals, and production.
```

Primary failure modes:

```text
Chaotic intake
Weak handoffs
Scope creep
Too many outputs
No clear approval owner
No learning loop
```

Agencies:

```text
Client Delivery AgencyOS
Creative Studio AgencyOS
Revenue AgencyOS
Risk and Proof AgencyOS
Insights AgencyOS
```

Configs:

```text
Client Delivery: Pod Model by client or project.
Creative Studio: Studio Model for recurring outputs and creative assets.
Revenue: Binary Pair with Delivery to keep offers sellable and fulfillable.
Risk and Proof: Governance Overlay for claims, scope, usage rights, approvals, and client promises.
Insights: Continuous Improvement Loop for revisions, conversion, client feedback, and reusable templates.
```

Example request:

```text
@operator I am a freelance brand designer. I have three client projects, need to post consistently, and want to raise my rates without overpromising. Help me organize the work.
```

Operator routing:

```text
Scope Gate: ongoing creative business operations with multiple workstreams.
Risk Gate: medium to high, because client promises, deadlines, usage rights, and pricing changes are involved.
Config Selector: Pod Model plus Studio Model plus Revenue <-> Delivery Binary plus Governance Overlay.
Agency Selector: Client Delivery owns client outcomes. Creative Studio owns production assets. Revenue owns positioning and offers. Risk and Proof checks claims and scope. Insights reviews what works.
```

Expected outputs:

```text
client project board
creative brief template
revision and approval workflow
proposal template
rate-increase positioning draft
content calendar
usage-rights and scope checklist
weekly review cadence
reusable asset library
```

How the agencies interact:

```text
Revenue AgencyOS drafts a higher-value offer.
Client Delivery AgencyOS checks whether the offer can be fulfilled.
Risk and Proof AgencyOS checks scope, claims, usage rights, and approval language.
Creative Studio AgencyOS Pro turns approved positioning into portfolio posts, case studies, and proposal assets.
Insights AgencyOS reviews which posts, proposals, and client types lead to better work.
```

Practical scenario:

```text
Client asks for "one extra version" after final approval.
Client Delivery checks project scope and approval history.
Risk and Proof checks whether the request is included or should become a paid change order.
Creative Studio estimates production impact.
Revenue drafts a polite upsell or change-order message.
Operator asks human owner to approve the response before sending.
```

Continuous improvement:

```text
After each project, Insights reviews revision count, client communication gaps, timeline accuracy, profitability, final asset reuse, and whether the brief or proposal template should change.
```

Closeout rule:

```text
Finished client work should become one of:
portfolio asset
case study
template improvement
lesson learned
archive
```
