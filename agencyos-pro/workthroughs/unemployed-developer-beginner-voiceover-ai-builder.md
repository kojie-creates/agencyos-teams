# Walkthrough: Unemployed Developer + Beginner Voice-Over Talent + AI-Assistant Builder

Scenario:

```text
An individual operator is unemployed, has software development ability, is beginning voice-over work, and uses AI assistants to build, market, and operate.
```

Brief confirmation:

```text
This is the polar opposite of FluidLogic's scenario.
FluidLogic had an existing company, product, proof base, and desired demographic transition.
This scenario starts with one person, limited cash, unclear market pull, mixed identity, and two possible earning paths.
The system must avoid overbuilding, fake polish, and premature brand strategy.
```

Strategic rule:

```text
Start cheap.
Find paid signal before identity polish.
Use AI assistants to compress research, outreach, asset creation, tracking, and iteration.
Do not let AI replace the human parts: performance, judgment, outreach, relationship building, and actual delivery.
```

Primary failure modes:

```text
Building instead of selling
Practicing without outreach
Calling beginner samples a professional demo reel
Applying broadly without a buyer thesis
Splitting attention across too many niches
Using AI to make generic portfolio filler
No follow-up loop
No evidence of reliability
No weekly continue/revise/stop decision
```

Agency shape:

```text
Central Orchestrator
Income Triage Agency
Market Signal Agency

Voice-Over Samples Agency
```

Configs:

```text
Voice-Over Samples: Studio Model
Outreach and Applications: Swarm Model
Delivery Operations: Pod Model

```

Operator routing:

```text
Scope Gate: personal income recovery with two early-stage earning paths.
Risk Gate: medium, because claims about skill, client readiness, employment fit, and audio quality must stay honest.
Config Selector: Hub-and-Spoke plus Board Model plus Studio Model plus Swarm Model plus Governance Overlay plus Continuous Improvement Loop.
Agency Selector: Central Orchestrator owns routing. Income Triage chooses where effort goes. Market Signal tests buyer demand. Builder Portfolio creates proof-of-work assets. Voice-Over Samples creates honest sample reads. Outreach and Applications starts conversations. Delivery Operations makes fulfillment reliable. Risk and Proof blocks overclaiming. Insights reviews response data.
Decision owner: the operator.
Definition of done: a narrow weekly plan, proof assets, outreach list, application/outreach scripts, response tracker, and learning loop are saved.
Automation rule: known handoffs run automatically. Pause only for human choice, recording/performance, personal preference, money commitment, public claim approval, or continue/revise/stop decisions.
```

## Required Inputs

The operator system needs these inputs before it can route work cleanly:

```text
cash runway
weekly available hours
current developer skill level
current voice-over sample quality
equipment available
remote/local work preference
minimum acceptable income
preferred buyer types
existing portfolio or GitHub links
existing audio samples
location constraints
confidence boundaries
claims that must remain honest
```

If an input is missing, the Orchestrator routes it as a research task or human gate.

## Phase 1: Central Orchestrator

Use first.

Purpose:

```text
Separate survival work, skill-building work, and long-term identity work.
Prevent the operator from treating all possible paths as equal.
Create one weekly route with measurable outputs.
```

Prompt:

```text
You are the Central Orchestrator for an unemployed software developer who is also a beginner voice-over talent and AI-assistant builder.

Create a routed work plan that prioritizes income signal.

Use these agencies:
Income Triage, Market Signal, Builder Portfolio, Voice-Over Samples, Outreach and Applications, Delivery Operations, Risk and Proof, Insights.

For each agency, define:
- job to be done
- required inputs
- output
- handoff recipient
- human gate
- evidence requirement

Keep the plan low-cost, honest, and execution-first.
Do not create a broad personal brand plan.
```

Produces:

```text
weekly routing map
priority order
proof asset list
outreach targets
human gates
tracking system
```

Hands off to:

```text
Income Triage Agency
Risk and Proof Agency
```

Automation behavior:

```text
When Phase 1 closes, Central Orchestrator automatically starts Income Triage and Risk and Proof in parallel.
Pause only if cash runway, weekly hours, or minimum acceptable income is unknown.
```

## Phase 2: Income Triage Agency

Use second.

Purpose:

```text
Choose the fastest ethical path to income signal.
Avoid treating voice-over, software, and AI-builder work as one blended pitch too early.
```

Prompt:

```text
You are the Income Triage Agency.

Compare three tracks:
1. software developer job search
2. small AI-assisted builder services
3. beginner voice-over service outreach

Rank them by:
- fastest realistic path to conversation
- fastest realistic path to paid work
- proof assets needed
- weekly effort required
- credibility risk
- emotional drag
- learning value

Recommend a primary track, secondary track, and parking lot.
```

Produces:

```text
track ranking
weekly effort split
primary offer hypothesis
secondary offer hypothesis
parking-lot list
human decision gate
```

Hands off to:

```text
Market Signal Agency
Builder Portfolio Agency
Voice-Over Samples Agency
```

Human gate:

```text
The operator chooses the weekly effort split.
```

## Phase 3: Market Signal Agency

Use in parallel with proof asset creation.

Purpose:

```text
Find buyer categories that match beginner-friendly, low-cost, fast-response opportunities.
```

Prompt:

```text
You are the Market Signal Agency.

Find practical buyer groups for:
- junior or contract software work
- AI-assisted workflow/build services
- beginner-friendly voice-over sample reads

Prioritize direct buyers, small businesses, production studios, agencies, founders, and local operators who value reliability and fast turnaround.

Avoid prestige-only targets that require expensive polish before first contact.

Produce a target list with:
- buyer type
- pain point
- offer angle
- proof needed
- contact channel
- first message angle
- disqualifier
```

Produces:

```text

lead list criteria

disqualifiers
proof gaps
```


## Phase 8: Risk and Proof Agency

Runs throughout.

Purpose:

```text
Keep the operator honest, credible, and protected from overclaiming.
```

Prompt:

```text
You are the Risk and Proof Agency.

Review all positioning, outreach, portfolio, resume, and voice-over language.

Separate:
- verified experience
- current capability
- beginner sample
- aspirational direction
- unsupported claim

Block language that implies:
- professional demo reel when only sample reads exist
- senior developer authority without evidence
- guaranteed business outcomes
- fake client results
- AI-built work without human accountability
```

Produces:

```text

approved language
blocked language
proof gaps
revision notes
```

Hands off to:

```text
Central Orchestrator
```

## Handoff Map

```text
Central Orchestrator
-> Income Triage + Risk and Proof in parallel
-> Market Signal
-> Builder Portfolio + Voice-Over Samples in parallel
-> Risk and Proof review
-> Outreach and Applications
-> Delivery Operations when conversations convert
-> Insights weekly
-> Central Orchestrator
-> automated revisions or human continue/revise/stop decision
```

## Automation Handoff Rules

| Trigger | Automatic action | Human pause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting inputs complete | Start Income Triage and Risk and Proof. | Cash runway, weekly hours, or minimum income unknown. |
| Track ranking complete | Start Market Signal for the chosen primary and secondary tracks. | Operator must choose effort split. |
| Buyer categories complete | Start proof assets for matching lanes. | Operator rejects a buyer type or niche. |
| Sample scripts complete | Ask operator to record. | Human performance required. |
| Proof assets complete | Start outreach and applications. | Public claim approval required. |
| Response arrives | Route to Delivery Operations or Insights. | Money, interview, or client commitment required. |
| Weekly report complete | Route revisions to active agencies. | Continue/revise/stop decision required. |

## Research To Income Workflow

```text
1. Central Orchestrator captures runway, time, skills, proof, and constraints.
2. Income Triage ranks software, builder services, and voice-over by income signal.
3. Market Signal identifies practical buyers and disqualifiers.
4. Builder Portfolio creates small proof-of-work assets.
5. Voice-Over Samples creates honest sample-read assets.
6. Risk and Proof approves language before outreach.
7. Outreach and Applications starts conversations.
8. Delivery Operations supports interviews, small projects, and voice-over delivery.
9. Insights reviews response data weekly.
10. Central Orchestrator routes revisions or asks for a human continue/revise/stop decision.
```

## Weekly Cadence

```text
Monday: choose the week's primary lane and outreach quota.
Tuesday: improve one proof asset.
Wednesday: send applications or outreach.
Thursday: follow up and record response data.
Friday: Insights reviews signal and recommends next week's route.
Before public updates: Risk and Proof checks claims.
```

## Closeout Rule

This scenario is complete only when the operator has evidence from the market:

```text
applications sent
outreach sent
follow-ups sent
responses tracked
proof assets improved
sample reads recorded
weekly decision made
next route chosen
```

The goal is not to resolve identity.
The goal is to create enough signal to choose the next useful move.
