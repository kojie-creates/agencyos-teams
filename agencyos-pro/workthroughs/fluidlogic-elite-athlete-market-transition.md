## Handoff Map

```text
Central Orchestrator
-> Research + Risk and Proof in parallel
-> Strategy and Positioning + Offer and Pilot Design as routed outputs mature
-> Sales Enablement + Content Studio in parallel after positioning approval
-> Risk and Proof review before public claims
-> Launch after pilot scope and claim approval
-> Insights from first outreach onward
-> Central Orchestrator
-> automated revisions or human continue/revise/stop decision
```

Risk and Proof runs across every public-facing phase.

Insights starts at first outreach, not after launch.

## Automation Handoff Rules

| Trigger | Automatic action | Human pause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief approved | Start Research and Risk and Proof in parallel. | Missing decision owner, unclear market goal, unresolved risk boundary. |
| Research output complete | Route opportunity map to Strategy and Positioning, Offer and Pilot Design, and Risk and Proof. | Segment choice requires Juan or commercial owner. |
| Claim matrix complete | Attach approved, needs-proof, and blocked language to Strategy, Sales Enablement, Content Studio, and Launch. | Claim is too strong for available evidence. |
| Positioning system complete | Start Offer and Pilot Design, Sales Enablement, and Content Studio asset planning. | Public-facing positioning approval required. |
| Pilot brief complete | Start sales scripts, outreach list, and launch tracker prep. | Pilot scope, budget, participant type, or success metric requires approval. |
| Outreach response arrives | Insights captures objections, language, segment signal, and pilot friction. | Customer response creates a risk, strategy fork, or contradiction. |
| Insights recommendation complete | Central Orchestrator routes revisions to the needed agencies. | Continue, revise, or stop decision required. |

Human required when:

```text
The system needs authority, not execution.
The input is missing and cannot be inferred safely.
Two agencies disagree on the route.
A claim touches performance, medical, safety, or athlete health outcomes.
The action commits budget, people, public messaging, or client promises.
The workflow reaches launch or continue/revise/stop.
```

## Research To GTM Workflow

```text
1. Research identifies high-pressure athlete segments.
2. Strategy turns those findings into premium positioning.
3. Risk and Proof checks claims before assets are built.
4. Offer and Pilot designs a narrow test.
5. Sales Enablement prepares direct conversations.
6. Content Studio creates only the assets needed for the test.
7. Launch runs targeted outreach and referral activation.
8. Insights reviews response and pilot behavior.
9. Central Orchestrator routes revisions.
10. Risk and Proof approves updated public claims.
```

## Small Team Operating Cadence

Weekly:

```text
Monday: Central Orchestrator sets priorities.
Tuesday: Research or Launch updates target list.
Wednesday: Sales Enablement reviews calls and objections.
Thursday: Content Studio updates assets only if learning supports it.
Friday: Insights publishes learning report.
Before public changes: Risk and Proof reviews claims.
```

Decision rules:

```text
Continue if a segment understands the value quickly and asks about pilot details.
Revise if interest exists but the use case or language is unclear.
Stop if the segment sees hydration as low urgency or cannot adopt the system operationally.
Escalate if any performance, medical, safety, or athlete health claim is stronger than the evidence.
```
